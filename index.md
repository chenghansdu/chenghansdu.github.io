Hi! I am a Phd candiate at Shandong University, supervised by Prof. Shijun Liu.

## Research Interest

I am interested in graph neural networks (GNNs), dynamic graph neural networks, and their applications in Artificial intelligence for IT operations (AIOps). My current research focuses on modeling the dynamic evolution of microservice systems using dynamic graphs to achieve more accurate root cause localization and thereby improve the reliability of microservice systems. In the future, I plan to extend dynamic graph neural networks to other scenarios, such as communication networks and base station networks. In addition, I am working on integrating dynamic graphs with large language models (LLMs) to develop root cause localization methods that are more interpretable, interactive, and user-friendly for operations engineers.
